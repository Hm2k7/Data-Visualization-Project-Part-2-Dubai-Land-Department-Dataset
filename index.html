Dubai Land Department Transactions

Data Visualization Project, Part 2 (Vega-Lite) - Group 3

All charts use transactions registered from 1 January 2015 to 31 August 2026.

Task 1: Transactions by Day of the Week

Number of transactions registered on each day of the week.

Task 1 Question: How many transactions fell on Fridays?

Answer: A total of 6,553 transactions fell on Fridays in between 1 January 2015 and 31 August 2026.

Explanation: The Friday numbers reflect the change to the official UAE weekend in January 2022, when the weekend moved from Friday–Saturday to Saturday–Sunday. Before 2022, Friday recorded only 299 transactions over seven years. After the change, Friday has become a half working day and has recorded 6,254 transactions. Saturdays and Sundays have zero transactions across the entire period, because DLD does not register transactions at the weekend.

Task 2: Monthly Sales, Off-Plan vs Existing Properties

Number of sales per month for off-plan and existing properties. Hover over the chart to see the numbers for a month.

Task 2 Question: In which month is the number of off-plan sales the highest, and how many off-plan sales is it?

Answer: September 2025 recorded the highest off-plan sales, with 706 sales.

Explanation: Tracking the orange line representing Off-Plan Properties from January 2015 to August 2026, the curve reaches its highest peak in September 2025, just above the 700 gridline on the y-axis. This point is highlighted and labelled on the chart as "Peak: September 2025 (706 sales)".

Task 3: Map of Prices Across Dubai's Communities

Median price per m² of unit and villa sales in each community. Hover over a community to see its name, number of sales and median price.

Task 3 Question: Which community has the largest number of sales of units and villas, and how many sales is it? Is this visualization effective in finding this community? Why?

Answer: Al Barsha South Fourth had the largest number of unit and villa sales, with 3,925 sales.

Explanation: When we hover over the communities on the map, the tooltip for Al Barsha South Fourth, shows 3,925 unit and villa sales with a median price of 12,923 AED/m², which is more than any other community. Then the next highest are Business Bay with 3,634 sales and Marsa Dubai with 2,955.

Why this is important: The map is not effective for finding this community. The colour of each community shows its median price per m², not its number of sales, so the number of sales can only be read from the tooltip. Al Barsha South Fourth is drawn in a mid-range blue which is the color for sales in the range of 11,000–14,000 AED/m² that it shares with 14 other communities, so nothing on the map makes it stand out, and we can't use shape since the area reflects that already. A bar chart sorted by number of sales would show it straight away though.
