Dubai Land Department Transactions

Data Visualization Project, Part 2 (Vega-Lite) - Group 3

All charts use transactions registered from 1 January 2015 to 31 August 2026.

Task 1: Transactions by Day of the Week

Number of transactions registered on each day of the week.

Answer

6,553 transactions were registered on Fridays.

Only 299 of them were before 2022. In January 2022 the UAE weekend moved from Friday-Saturday to Saturday-Sunday and Friday became a half working day, so Friday registrations went up to 6,254 from 2022 onwards. There are no transactions at all on Saturdays or Sundays.

Dates are read as UTC (utc:'%Y-%m-%d' and utcday), so the day of the week does not change with the time zone of the computer viewing the chart.

Task 2: Monthly Sales, Off-Plan vs Existing Properties

Number of sales per month for off-plan and existing properties. Hover over the chart to see the numbers for a month.

Answer

September 2025, with 706 off-plan sales.
  • We used a line chart because the data is a time series, and two lines make it easy to compare off-plan and existing sales over time.
  • The x-axis covers every month from January 2015 to August 2026 and is labelled by year.
  • Off-plan sales have been higher than existing sales in every month since January 2024.

Task 3: Map of Prices Across Dubai's Communities

Median price per m² of unit and villa sales in each community. Hover over a community to see its name, number of sales and median price.

Answer

Al Barsha South Fourth (Jumeirah Village Circle), with 3,925 sales.

It is followed by Business Bay (3,634) and Marsa Dubai (2,955). Its median price is 12,923 AED/m².

Is the map effective for finding it? No. The colour shows the median price, not the number of sales, so the number of sales is only in the tooltip and you would have to hover over every community to find the highest one. Bigger shapes also get more attention, but the size of a shape is just its land area: the large desert communities stand out even though most of them have no sales, while Al Barsha South Fourth is a normal-sized, mid-priced community. A bar chart sorted by number of sales would show it straight away.

Design choices

  • Communities with fewer than 10 sales are left grey, because a median of only a few sales is not reliable.
  • The prices are skewed: most communities are between about 11,000 and 25,000 AED/m², but a few go up to 75,000. So instead of a linear scale we used 5 colour classes with breaks at 11,000, 14,000, 18,000 and 25,000, which puts about 15 communities in each colour.
  • All 226 communities are drawn in grey first, so communities with no sales are still visible on the map.
  • Mercator projection, fitted to the community boundaries so the whole emirate is shown.
